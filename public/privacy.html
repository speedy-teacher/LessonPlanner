🎵 Lesson Planner

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

In short: Lesson Planner is a free website with no servers, databases, accounts or tracking of its own. Your plans are kept in your own web browser and, if you connect it, in your own Google Drive. The people who publish this site never receive, see, store, sell or share your plans or your Google information.

1. Who this policy is about

"Lesson Planner" (the "App") is a free, open-source planning tool for music teachers, published as a static website on GitHub Pages by an individual volunteer (the "Publisher", "we", "us"). "You" means anyone who opens or uses the App. By using the App you agree to this policy and to the Terms of Use.

2. The App has no server of its own

The App is a set of files (web pages and program code) sent to your browser by GitHub Pages. Everything the App does happens inside your browser. The Publisher runs no server, database, account system, analytics, advertising or tracking, and has no way to see what you type or save in the App.

3. What is kept, and where

  • In your browser on your computer. Your plans, recently opened plans, view settings and (if you choose) access to folders on your computer are saved in your browser's own storage (IndexedDB and localStorage). This stays on that computer. You can remove it at any time by removing the plan in the App or clearing the site's data in your browser settings.
  • In your Google Drive, if you connect it. Your plan files are saved in a folder you choose in your own Google Drive account. They are controlled by you and by Google's terms, and they are shared only if you share them yourself.
  • Files you download. If you click "Download a copy", the file goes to your computer and is yours.
  • Lesson copies, if you turn them on. If you choose a folder for lesson copies, the App makes a copy of a lesson's file for each week it's on your Lesson Schedule, in that folder of your own Google Drive (or a folder on your computer), and types in details such as the school, grade and week. Small hidden labels on each copy (only this App can read them) remember which lesson and week it belongs to. Your original lesson files are never changed.

The Publisher keeps no copy of any of this.

4. Google sign-in and Google Drive

Connecting Google Drive is optional. If you connect, you sign in on Google's own page, and Google gives your browser a short-lived access pass (about one hour). That pass is kept only in your browser's memory while the page is open. It is never saved, and never sent to the Publisher or anyone else.

The App asks Google for these permissions:

  • See, edit, create and delete only the Google Drive files you use with this app (drive.file) — to save and open your plan files, and to work with files you pick or the App creates.
  • See and download your Google Drive files (drive.readonly) — so the App can list the lesson files and pictures inside folders you choose and show their names and a small preview.

Pictures in lesson copies. When a lesson copy gets a picture (for example a bitmoji) from your Drive, Google can only put it into the slide if Google is able to fetch it. So, for that picture only, the App turns on "Anyone with the link can view" for a few seconds while Google copies it into the slide, then turns that sharing off again. The link is never shown or sent anywhere else, but during those seconds anyone who already had the link could view the picture. Pictures you have shared that way yourself are left as they are. If you'd rather this never happen, don't give your lessons a picture spot.

The App uses this access only to show and save your own lesson planning in your browser, at your request. From your Drive files it remembers only lesson details (such as a file's name, type and link) inside your plan. It does not copy the contents of your files anywhere outside your own Google Drive (or your own computer). Google information is never sold, never used for advertising, never used to train artificial-intelligence models, and never transferred to anyone.

Lesson Planner's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can disconnect at any time by closing the App and removing "Lesson Planner" at your Google Account's third-party connections page.

5. Other companies involved

Opening the App means your browser contacts some outside companies, as any website does. They may record basic technical information such as your internet (IP) address, browser type and the time of the visit, under their own privacy policies. The Publisher does not receive this information and is not responsible for these companies.

  • GitHub (hosts the website) — GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Google (sign-in, Google Drive, file previews and the page's font) — Google Privacy Policy

6. Student and personal information

The App is a planning tool for adults (teachers). It is not meant for children and is not meant to hold student records. Please do not enter student names or other personal details about students or anyone else. If you choose to, you are responsible for following your school's, district's and the law's rules (for example FERPA or COPPA in the United States, or similar laws elsewhere). Because the Publisher never receives your data, the Publisher cannot see, correct, export or delete it for you — you control it directly in your browser and your Google Drive.

7. Cookies

The App itself sets no cookies and uses no tracking. It uses your browser's storage only to keep your plans and settings, as described above. Google may use its own cookies while you sign in.

8. Security

Your information is protected by your browser, your computer and your Google account. Keep them secure, use a strong password, and close the App on shared computers. No method of storing or sending information is perfectly secure, and the Publisher cannot guarantee security. See the Terms of Use.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may change at any time. The new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Using the App after a change means you accept the new version.

10. Questions

The App's code is public. Questions can be asked by opening an issue at github.com/speedy-teacher/LessonPlanner/issues (please don't post personal information there — issues are public).
